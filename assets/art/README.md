# Mate art

Sources are in `src/` (HTML/SVG drawn with `src/mate-lib.js`); `./mm art` renders them to the PNG files here with headless Edge. Concept art for the README, the website and social posts; the in-app robot will be layered SVG in `assets/robot/`.

| Image | Alt text |
|---|---|
| `mate.png` (transparent) | Mate, a friendly light bulb with a face, shining his flashlight to the right |
| `mate-sheet.png` | Meet Mate: the light bulb in idle, listening, pointing and proud poses, with the colour palette |
| `pixel-sprites.png` | Pixel art: MATE, your screen buddy, a light bulb in four poses: idle, listen, point, happy |
| `pixel-save.png` | Pixel art: Mate shines a beam of light on the SAVE button and says "Right here, mate!" |
| `meme-right-here.png` | Meme: "Where's the Save button?" / "Right here, mate." Mate shines his light toward a glowing save icon |
| `meme-nah-yeah.png` | Meme: Mate refuses "an AI that clicks everything for you" and likes "an AI that shows you where to click" |
| `meme-grandson.png` | Meme: a grandson's confusing phone directions vs. Mate flying over and shining his light on it |
| `avatar-options.png` | The four looks we considered (owl, bulb, cursor arrow, retro monitor) and the earlier round robot |
| `bulb-options.png` | Three refinements of the bulb; B1 "classic glow" was chosen |
