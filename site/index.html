How it works

A patient teacher on the screen, not an agent that takes the mouse away.

  1. 1

    Ask aloud

    Hold Right Ctrl—or tap in hands-free mode—and ask in plain words, such as “How do I make the text bigger?”

  2. 2

    Mate flies to the button and lights it

    Mate glides across the screen to the exact button and shines a warm golden ring around it, giving one clear step at a time.

  3. 3

    You click, he cheers

    Your parent clicks the button themselves. Mate checks that it was the right spot, cheers them on, and moves to the next step.

Safety and privacy

He never presses Pay or Delete. He only shows the way.

  • Never clicks Pay, Delete, or passwords

    Mate points out where to click and waits. He never clicks payment, purchase, delete, money transfer, or password buttons.

  • One verified click only when asked

    In a lesson, if your parent asks Mate to press a safe button for them, he asks “Should I press this button for you? Say yes or no.” and performs at most one left click per spoken “yes”.

  • Only the active window, only during a question

    A screenshot is taken only when your parent asks a question, and includes only the app window they are asking about and its pop-up menus.

  • Passwords blacked out before leaving the PC

    Password fields, gaps, and every other app’s window are painted black on the computer before anything is sent. If Mate cannot guarantee a safe view, he fails closed and refuses to look.

  • Honest when he cannot find something

    If a button is not on screen, Mate says “I can’t find it” rather than pointing at a guess.

  • Never steals focus or exposes keys

    Mate’s overlay windows never take focus away from your parent’s app. Provider keys stay on the proxy server, never inside the desktop app.
