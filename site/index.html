Mate lights the button. Your parent clicks it.

Mate is a small light-bulb helper for Windows who flies to the right button in any app, guides your parent step by step, and waits for them to click—never pressing Pay or Delete.

Download for Windows Windows 10 & 11 · Holds Right Ctrl or taps to talk

How it works

A patient teacher on the screen, not an agent that takes the mouse away.

  1. 1

    Ask aloud

    Hold Right Ctrl—or tap in hands-free mode—and ask in plain words, such as “How do I make the text bigger?” What Mate hears appears live in his bubble while you speak.

  2. 2

    Mate flies to the button and lights it

    Mate glides across the screen to the exact button and shines a warm golden ring around it, giving one clear step at a time.

  3. 3

    You click, he cheers

    Your parent clicks the button themselves. Mate checks that it was the right spot, cheers them on, and moves to the next step.

Keepsake lesson cards

A printed reminder to keep by the keyboard, written simply and painted in soft gouache.

Simple steps in your language

After a lesson the model finished, Muse Glimmer 30B (Meta open weights on our own Modal L4 GPU) rewrites the steps in simple words in the person’s language and writes a short scene to accompany it.

Soft gouache picture, ready to print

Muse Image draws that scene in soft gouache with Mate in a corner and no text in the picture. The card window displays the artwork alongside the steps as text, with Print and Close buttons, and saves a PNG in Pictures\MetaMate.

Scam check: “Is this safe?”

A calm second opinion when an unfamiliar message, email, or pop-up looks suspicious.

Clear verdict with mood and red flags

Mate looks only at the current window, delivers a verdict with a mood, and names up to 3 red flags he actually sees. Using Muse Spark 1.3, he shines his flashlight beam directly at the worst one.

Never clicks and never urges action

Mate never clicks anything on screen and never tells the person to click a link, call a phone number, or pay. He shows what is there so your parent can stay safe without being rushed.

Safety and privacy

He never presses Pay or Delete. He only shows the way.

  • Never clicks Pay, Delete, or passwords

    Mate points out where to click and waits. He never clicks payment, purchase, delete, money transfer, or password buttons.

  • One verified click only when asked

    In a lesson, if your parent asks Mate to press a safe button for them, he asks “Should I press this button for you? Say yes or no.” and performs at most one left click per spoken “yes”.

  • Only the active window, only during a question

    A screenshot is taken only when your parent asks a question, and includes only the app window they are asking about and its pop-up menus.

  • Passwords blacked out before leaving the PC

    Password fields, gaps, and every other app’s window are painted black on the computer before anything is sent. If Mate cannot guarantee a safe view, he fails closed and refuses to look.

  • Honest when he cannot find something

    If a button is not on screen, Mate says “I can’t find it” rather than pointing at a guess.

  • Never steals focus or exposes keys

    Mate’s overlay windows never take focus away from your parent’s app. Provider keys stay on the proxy server, never inside the desktop app.
