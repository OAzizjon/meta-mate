14 Elm Road
Riverton
31 October 2026

Riverton Borough Council
Highways Department

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing about the street lamp outside number 14 on Elm Road, which has not worked since the middle of September. The pavement there is uneven, and in the dark evenings it is difficult for me and my neighbours to walk safely to the bus stop.

I would be grateful if you could arrange for the lamp to be repaired as soon as possible.

Yours faithfully,
Margaret Lane
