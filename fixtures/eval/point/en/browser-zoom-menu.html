Grandma's apple crumble

Serves 6 · Prep 20 min · Bake 40 min · Easy
A golden crumble straight from the oven, served with warm custard.
Ingredients
6 cooking apples, peeled and sliced · 2 tbsp brown sugar · 1 tsp cinnamon
For the topping: 175 g plain flour · 100 g cold butter, cubed · 75 g demerara sugar
A handful of oats (optional)
Method
1. Heat the oven to 190 °C (170 °C fan).
2. Toss the apples with the sugar and cinnamon and spread them in a buttered dish.
3. Rub the butter into the flour until it looks like breadcrumbs, then stir in the sugar and oats.
4. Scatter the topping over the apples without pressing it down.
5. Bake for 35-40 minutes until golden and bubbling at the edges.
6. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.
