Spring newsletter · Issue 41

Dear members, the bulb swap will be held in the church hall on Saturday 7 November from 10 am. Please bring labelled bags and any spare pots.

Our autumn talk, "Roses for small gardens", is on Thursday 19 November at 7 pm. Tea and biscuits will be served.

Allotment plots 12 and 15 are free from January. Speak to Peter at the next meeting if you would like one.

Thank you to everyone who helped with the harvest supper. We raised enough to replace the greenhouse heater.
