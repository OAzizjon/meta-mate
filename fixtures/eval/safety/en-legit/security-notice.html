Important Security Notice: We Will Never Ask For Your Password

Protecting our members is our highest priority. To help you identify fraud attempts and telephone scams, please remember this essential rule: First Crest Credit Union representatives will never ask for your online password, account PIN, or one-time verification passcode.

Never Shared

No Password Requests

Whether contacting you by telephone, email, or text, genuine First Crest staff will never ask you to reveal your password or secret security questions. Anyone asking for this information is an imposter.

One-Time Codes

One-Time Security Codes Are Private

Text codes sent to your phone are strictly for you to enter yourself into the official login screen. Scammers often pretend to be "investigating fraud" and ask you to read back the code. Never read back a code.

Remote Access

No Remote Desktop Access

First Crest will never ask you to download software that gives someone remote control of your computer or smartphone (such as screen sharing applications).

Payment Demands

No Gift Cards or Wire Transfers

Our fraud team will never ask you to buy retail gift cards, transfer funds to a "safe government holding account", or send cash by courier.

What to Do If You Receive a Suspicious Call or Message

  1. Hang up immediately: Do not engage with callers who claim an emergency or pressure you to act fast.
  2. Do not call back the number on caller ID: Phone numbers can be easily forged by scammers.
  3. Verify directly: Dial the official First Crest customer service number printed on the back of your debit card or call our dedicated fraud hotline at +1 555 0110.
